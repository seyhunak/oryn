# Code of conduct

ORYN is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
We want taking part to be a good experience for every one of them.

## What we expect

- Be considerate and respectful in issues, pull requests, reviews and chat.
- Critique code and ideas, never people. Assume good intent and ask before assuming.
- Give and accept review with patience; explain the reason behind a request.
- Credit other people's work.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What we do not accept

- Harassment, intimidation or discrimination of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repository, issues, discussions, chat) and when
someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub's private
contact options on the repository (or the security advisory form for anything that is
also a security matter). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove
content, and warn, suspend or ban participants, in proportion to what happened.
